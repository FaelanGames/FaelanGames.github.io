This website

faelangames.com

This site is a set of static pages. We do not run analytics, advertising or tracking scripts on it, and we do not set cookies.

Your theme choice (dark or light) is kept in your own browser's local storage so the site remembers it between visits. It never leaves your device and we cannot read it.

The site loads its display typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser makes a request to Google when a page opens. Links out to Google Play, our social profiles and Ko-fi take you to those companies' own sites, where their privacy policies apply.

The site is published through GitHub Pages, which keeps server logs of requests as part of hosting the site.

If you email us, we keep the message so we can reply to you. Questions about any of this can go to contact@faelangames.com.
