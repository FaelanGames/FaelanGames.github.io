Endless Color Sort

Pour colourful liquids from bottle to bottle until every bottle holds a single colour. Play one level or stay for an hour. It picks up where you left off.

  • 5,000 levels From quick warm-ups to puzzles worth sitting with.
  • Daily Challenge A new one every day, with a bottle to earn each month. Missed days stay open.
  • 41 bottle styles, 23 backgrounds To collect as you play.
  • No forced ads No ads interrupt you between levels. Rewarded ads are optional.

Two people, one studio

Faelan Games is the two of us. We plan and build our games together, from the first idea to the version we eventually share with players.

We started with the kind of game we actually reach for at the end of a day: calm, easy to pick up, and not constantly interrupting you. Endless Color Sort came out of that, and the same thinking runs through the games we're working on now.

We build for Android first, release a game when we're happy with it, and keep improving it afterwards. Keeping the studio small means feedback comes straight to us, and we stay closely involved in every decision about the games we make.
