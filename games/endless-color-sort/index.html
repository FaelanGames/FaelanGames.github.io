Endless Color Sort

Pour colourful liquids from bottle to bottle until every bottle holds a single matching colour, across 5,000 levels of relaxing sorting puzzles.

Android Available now No forced ads
Get it on Google Play Watch the launch video

The game

What's in it

5,000 levels

Enough puzzles to last, from quick warm-ups to boards worth sitting with. Play a single level or keep going. Your progress is saved either way.

Daily challenges

A fresh puzzle every day, with a monthly bottle reward to work towards. Missed a day? Earlier challenges stay available.

Make it yours

Collect 41 bottle styles and unlock 23 backgrounds as you play. Choose your favourites and give your puzzles a different look.

No forced ads

No banners or interruptions between levels. You can choose to watch an ad for an extra reward, or keep playing without one.
