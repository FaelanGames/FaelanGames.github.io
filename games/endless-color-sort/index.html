About the game

Sort, organise and solve.

Turn a rack of mixed bottles into a neat set of matching colours, one careful pour at a time.

Endless Color Sort is a relaxing colour-sorting puzzle game for Android. Tap one bottle, then another, to pour the colour on top, and work out the order of moves that leaves every bottle holding a single colour.

The game ships with 2,000 fixed puzzles. Every player gets the same numbered levels in the same order, so a level you are stuck on is a level someone else can help you with. What starts as simple sorting gradually adds bottles, colours and tighter solutions, and some levels keep a colour hidden until it is poured.

When a level will not fall into place, three powers are there to help: Undo to take a pour back, Shuffle to rearrange what is in front of you, and Extra Bottle to give yourself room to work. Numbered levels can also be skipped outright if you would rather move on. Daily Challenges are meant to be solved as they come, so Skip is not available there.

Features

More than one puzzle at a time

Alongside the numbered levels there is a daily puzzle to come back to, a collection to build and a look you can make your own.

Daily Challenges

Daily Challenges unlock as soon as Level 20 becomes reachable, and then bring a fresh puzzle every day. Miss a day and nothing is lost: earlier Daily Challenges stay available to play later.

Monthly rewards

Daily Challenges are grouped by calendar month, with a bottle reward for the month to work towards as the days fill in.

39 bottle styles

Collect bottle styles as you play and choose the set your puzzles are poured into, from plain glass to something considerably less plain.

Unlockable backgrounds

Backgrounds unlock as you progress, so the scene behind the puzzle changes along with the collection you are building.

Light, dark or system

Choose a Light or Dark theme, or let the game follow whatever your device is already set to.

Hidden colours

Some levels keep a colour hidden until it is poured, turning a tidy sorting puzzle into a question of what to risk first.

Advertising

No forced ads

Endless Color Sort never interrupts your game with an advertisement.

There are no ads on startup, no banners, no interstitials between levels and no ads on a timer.

The only advertisements in the game are optional rewarded ads, and one is never shown unless you ask for it. You might choose to watch one to top a power back up, to skip a numbered level, to multiply a reward you have just earned or to repair a broken streak.

Turning one down simply means the extra is not granted. Ordinary play is unaffected, and the puzzles remain playable if an advertisement cannot be loaded at all.

Endless Color Sort has no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.
