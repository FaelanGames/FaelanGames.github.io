Endless Color Sort

Pour colourful liquids from bottle to bottle until every bottle holds a single matching colour, across 2,000 levels of relaxing sorting puzzles.

Collect 39 bottle styles and unlock backgrounds as you play. Choose your favourites and give your puzzles a different look.

No forced ads

No banners or interruptions between levels. You can choose to watch an ad for an extra reward, or keep playing without one.
