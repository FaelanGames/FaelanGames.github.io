About the game

Grow a settlement from the ground up.

Manage people, resources and construction while your community develops over time.

Mobile Strategy is the current working title for a settlement-building strategy game focused on creating and developing a living community.

Gather and manage resources, construct buildings and support the people who live within your settlement.

As your population grows, new needs, opportunities and challenges will shape how the settlement develops.

Planned for Android

Mobile Strategy is currently in development for Android and is planned for release through Google Play.

The game is earlier in development than some of our other projects. Its name and details may change significantly as development continues.

Screenshots and gameplay images will be added as development continues.
